Using yeast to understand aging and how cells sort their membranes

We use the budding yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae to study aging and how lipid transport and membrane traffic affect lifespan.

Explore our research

About the lab

We focus on how cells move lipids and proteins between their membranes and organelles, especially the vacuole, and how this traffic affects cell health and lifespan.

Yeast shares many of its core cell-biology machines with human cells, so genetic experiments in yeast can quickly test ideas about human aging and disease.
