Principal investigator

FT

Fusheng Tang, Ph.D.

Principal Investigator · Associate Professor, Department of Biology, UA Little Rock

Research interests: molecular biology of aging, intracellular sterol transport and cellular systems biology. Dr. Tang uses yeast as a model to study aging and the membrane-trafficking pathways that affect lifespan.

Training, short bio, email, links to Google Scholar and ORCID.

Graduate students

OJ

Omotolani Jimoh

PhD Candidate in Biology

Omotolani studies how Osh6 changes traffic between the trans-Golgi network, late endosomes, and the vacuole, including in mutants of the retromer subunit Vps35, whose human counterpart is linked to Parkinson's disease. She built the strain collection that combines Osh6 overexpression with trafficking mutants, established the lab's CPY secretion western blot assay, and quantifies GFP-Snc1 trafficking by microscopy. She also mentors undergraduates in the molecular biology laboratory course.
