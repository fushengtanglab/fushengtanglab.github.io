Service: fluorescence microscopy

Our lab runs a fluorescence and DIC microscope for live-cell imaging, and we help researchers from other laboratories image their samples.

The microscope

  • Microscope: Nikon widefield fluorescence microscope with differential interference contrast (DIC)
  • Objectives: 10×, 20×, 40×, 60× and 100× (Nikon Plan Apo VC 100× oil DIC N2, NA 1.4)
  • Camera: Nikon DS-Qi2 monochrome camera (0.073 µm per pixel with the 100× objective)
  • Software: Nikon NIS-Elements for acquisition; image processing and quantification in Fiji/ImageJ.

How we help other labs

We help students from other laboratories image their samples, including tissue sections and cultured cells. We take fluorescence and DIC images for their projects across the full range of objectives, from 10× overviews to 100× high-resolution images.

Researchers interested in using the microscope can contact Dr. Tang.
