Research

To accommodate the fragmented time of undergraduate researchers, we employ genetics and systems biology approaches. Currently, we focus on the following two research topics.

Project 1

Identifying lipids that remedy vacuolar membranes

Osh6 counter-transports phosphatidylserine (PS) and phosphatidylinositol-4-phosphate (PI4P) between closely contacted membranes. We found that up-regulation of Osh6 accumulates PI4P on a special subset of the trans-Golgi network (TGN) (Kadhim et al., 2022). This accumulation likely enhances vesicle traffic from the TGN to late endosomes (LEs), which fuse into vacuoles. We are currently measuring the extent to which blocking PI4P and PS metabolism decreases LE-vacuole fusion and vacuole-vacuole fusion in our Osh6 longevity mutant.

Project 2

Identifying lipids that allocate TORC1

The target of rapamycin complex 1 (TORC1) is a master kinase for cell growth. Recent studies in yeast cells reveal that vacuolar TORC1 (vTORC1) stimulates protein synthesis, while endosomal TORC1 (eTORC1) represses autophagy, a degradative pathway toward vacuoles that extends the lifespan. Our longevity mutant of Osh6 partially down-regulates TORC1 but does not compromise cell growth (Gebre et al., 2012). Currently, we are measuring the extent to which mutating the TGN to LE traffic decreases autophagy and the cell size enlargement of our Osh6 longevity mutant.

Techniques for students to learn

  • Yeast genetics: gene deletions, chromosomal promoter replacement (e.g., PERG6-OSH6) and combined mutant strains, confirmed by PCR.
  • Systems biology: identification of genes co-clustered with OSH6 from genome-wide expression profiles, gene-gene, and protein-protein interaction networks.
  • Live-cell fluorescence microscopy: GFP-tagged proteins such as Snc1 and Pma1, FM4-64 to follow endocytosis and vacuoles, and actin staining.
  • Protein sorting and secretion assays: detecting the vacuolar enzyme carboxypeptidase Y (CPY) in its native form secreted by colonies onto a membrane laid on the plate (colony dot blot), and in denatured cell extracts and growth medium (western blot), which separates the precursor and mature forms of CPY.
  • Growth and stress assays: spot assays and calcofluor white sensitivity.
  • Aging and systems biology (earlier work): lifespan measurements and computational approaches to find longevity genes and model cellular processes.
